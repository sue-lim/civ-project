About

Hi there 👋 I'm Sue, currently a student with SheCodes Australia as part of the 2022/2023 cohort, and a current student at TAFE North Metro completing a Certificate IV in Programming. This journey has been exciting, challenging, and incredibly rewarding—I still have lots to learn, but I’m eager to keep going and growing.

My passion for technology started early. I’ve always been curious about how things work behind the scenes and spent time experimenting with VBA, HTML, and anything tech-related I could explore. Now, I’m taking that early interest further through formal study and hands-on projects.

As part of my learning, I’m exploring a wide range of technologies, including HTML, CSS, Python, JavaScript, React, Django REST Framework (DRF), RESTful APIs, SQL, and C#. Each new concept and project helps me build more confidence and deepen my understanding of software development.

Growing up, I loved computers 💻, but I also doubted whether I belonged in a field that historically lacked gender diversity. Today, the landscape is changing, and I’m proud to be part of a growing movement of women entering tech. I truly believe diverse perspectives lead to more innovation, richer collaboration, and better outcomes.

Feel free to explore my projects—your feedback and suggestions are always appreciated as I continue my journey as a developer.
